A kitten of your own to look after.

Whiskerling is a virtual kitten for iPad. Feed it, wash it, play with it and put it to bed, and over a week it grows into a cat. There are no ads and no account, and nothing leaves the iPad.

Gentle, on purpose.

It never dies

A cat that has been forgotten for a while is only sad until someone looks after it again. Nobody is punished for a busy week.

Its day goes on

Time passes while the app is closed. After school the kitten is hungry, and in the morning it's rested, because it sleeps at night.

Nothing pushy

No ads, no chat and no nagging. The one purchase sits behind a gate that asks a grown-up for a sum.

A day with your kitten.

Feed it, wash it, put it to bed

Kibble, fish or a treat. A sponge bath when it gets grubby, a clean litter box, and lights out when it's tired. Stroke it and it purrs; tap it and it tells you what it needs.

Out in the garden Pro

A pond, an apple tree and butterflies, with stars and fireflies at night. At the pond the kitten plays Catch the fish: tap the fish as they jump, and it leaps after them. Nobody loses.

Hearts, and something to wear

Looking after the kitten earns hearts. They're never spent. They unlock a bow, a bell, flowers, sunglasses, a top hat, a scarf and a crown, and there are six coats to choose from.

It grows up

A kitten for the first two days, a young cat until day six, then a grown-up cat. The clock on the wall shows the real time, and the sky in the window follows the day.

For the grown-ups.

No ads, no account

Nothing to sign up for and nobody to chat with. Purchases, settings and every link out of the game sit behind a grown-up gate.

Free to play. Pro is one purchase

The whole care game is free. Whiskerling Pro, one purchase with no subscription, adds four coats, five accessories, the garden and Catch the fish. What is added to Pro later, like new cats and games, is included.

Data Not Collected

The app has no analytics and sends nothing anywhere. The kitten is saved on the iPad only.

Whiskerling started as a little web game I made for an old iPad at home, one that can't run new apps any more. I rebuilt it as a proper app for newer iPads and kept what mattered most: no ads, nothing to buy by accident, and a cat that is never punished for a busy week. It's open source, so anyone can see exactly what it does.

Questions.

Is Whiskerling free?

Yes. The whole care game is free, with two coats, the bow, the bell and the feather. Whiskerling Pro is an optional one-time purchase, with no subscription, that adds four coats, five accessories, the garden and Catch the fish. What is added to Pro later, like new cats and games, is included.

Can my child buy something by accident?

No. Buying and restoring Pro, the settings and every link out of the game sit behind a gate that asks for a multiplication, such as 7 × 8.

Does Whiskerling collect any data?

No. The app has no ads, no analytics, no account and no tracking. The kitten is saved on the iPad and nowhere else. The only network traffic is Apple's own, for the purchase. The privacy policy has the details.

Can the cat die?

No, and it can't run away either. If it's forgotten for a while it gets hungry and sad, and it's happy again as soon as someone looks after it.

Which iPads does it run on?

Any iPad with iPadOS 17 or later. It works offline. There's no iPhone version.

Which languages is it in?

English and Danish. The app follows the iPad's language.
