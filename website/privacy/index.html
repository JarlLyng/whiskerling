Privacy policy

Last updated 15 November 2026

Whiskerling is a game for children, and it collects nothing about them or about you. This page says exactly what that means, for the app and for this website.

The app

Whiskerling collects no personal data and no usage data. It has no analytics, no advertising, no crash reporting and no account, and it contains no code from third parties. The App Store privacy label says Data Not Collected.

The kitten, its name, how it's doing and the hearts it has earned are saved on the iPad only, in the app's own storage. They leave the iPad only as part of the iPad's own backups, to iCloud or a computer, which you and Apple control. The name a child types for the cat stays on the iPad too.

The app makes no network connections of its own. When a grown-up buys or restores Whiskerling Pro, Apple's App Store handles the purchase. The app never sees payment details or who bought it, only whether Pro is unlocked on this iPad. Apple's own privacy policy covers the purchase.

Behind the grown-ups' gate

"Send feedback" opens a draft email to support@iamjarl.com with the app's version and the iPadOS version filled in. Nothing is sent unless you send it. If you do, I receive your email address and what you wrote, and I use them only to answer you. "Rate Whiskerling" uses Apple's own rating prompt.

Children

Whiskerling is made for children and follows Apple's rules for apps in the Kids category: no third-party analytics or advertising, and purchases and every link out of the app sit behind a parental gate that asks for a multiplication.

This website

whiskerling.iamjarl.com sets no cookies and runs no analytics or tracking scripts. Every file, fonts included, is served from the site itself. The site is hosted on GitHub Pages, and GitHub may log visitors' IP addresses for security, as its privacy statement describes.

Changes

If anything here changes, this page changes first, with a new date. The app's source code is open, so anyone can check what it does.

Who is responsible
