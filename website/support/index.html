Support

Write to support@iamjarl.com. I read every mail and answer as soon as I can.

From inside the app it's easiest to use the grown-ups' menu: choose "Send feedback", and the mail has the app's version and the iPadOS version filled in already.

How do I open the grown-ups' menu?

Hold your finger on the cat's name at the top left for two seconds, then answer the multiplication. The menu has buying and restoring Pro, sound, feedback, the privacy policy and starting over.

I bought Pro, but it's still locked

Open the grown-ups' menu and choose "Restore purchase". Use the same Apple Account that bought it. Pro also works through Family Sharing.

How do I start over with a new cat?

In the grown-ups' menu, choose "Start over with a new cat" and tap it once more to confirm. The old cat can't be brought back afterwards.

The cat is hungry and sad after a few days away

That's how it should be: time goes on while the app is closed. Feed it, play with it and give it a cuddle, and it's happy again. It never dies and never runs away.

There's no sound

Check the speaker button at the top right of the game. Whiskerling also stays quiet when the iPad's silent mode is on.

Does it need the internet?

No. Only buying or restoring Pro needs a connection to the App Store.

Found a bug?

A mail is perfect. If you use GitHub, you can also open an issue.
